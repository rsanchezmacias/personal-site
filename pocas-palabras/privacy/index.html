Pocas Palabras

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Pocas Palabras is a personal journaling and life planning app. Your privacy is important. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data Storage

All data you create in Pocas Palabras — including journal entries, todos, reminders, and settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. No data is transmitted to any external server.

Data We Do Not Collect

Pocas Palabras does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No account or login required
  • No data shared with third parties
  • No advertising

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Pocas Palabras requests permission to send local notifications. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external service.

Quotes

The daily quotes shown in the app are bundled locally with the app. No network request is made to fetch them.

Age Requirement

Pocas Palabras is intended for users aged 13 and older. By using the app, you confirm that you meet this age requirement.

Deleting Your Data

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling Pocas Palabras. This permanently removes all journal entries, todos, reminders, and settings from your device.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
